Desk mapper

Load your floor plan, click each desk position, and name it. Drag a marker to nudge it, shift-click to delete. Coordinates are stored as percentages, so the map stays correct at any screen size. When you are done, copy the rows into the Desks tab of the Google Sheet, and save the same image as docs/assets/floorplan.png in the repo.
